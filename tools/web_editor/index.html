Forbidden Workshop

Make your own mods for the iPhone port of Yu-Gi-Oh! Forbidden Memories: opponents' decks, what they drop, and card pictures.

Starting the editor…

Choose your own Forbidden Memories (USA) disc image, the .bin of its .bin/.cue pair. It stays on your phone: the workshop reads the card tables out of it and keeps them in this browser.

The game's copy is in Files › On My iPhone › Forbidden Memories › game.

Put it in the game

  1. Save the .zip to Files.
  2. In Files, tap it once to unpack it into a folder.
  3. Move that folder into On My iPhone › Forbidden Memories › mods.
  4. In the game, open MENU › Game › Mods and turn it on.
